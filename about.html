Who we are

Rooted in Christ, reaching our community.

We are a church family committed to worship, discipleship, service, and compassion.

Our mission

Jesimiel Revival Ministries exists to help people experience the love of God, grow in their faith, and serve others with courage and kindness.

We believe the church should be a place of belonging for everyone, from the newest visitor to the lifelong believer.

What we believe

  • God is the creator and sustainer of life.
  • Jesus Christ is the risen Savior.
  • The Bible is the living word of God.
  • Faith should transform everyday life.

Our values

Faith, family, and service guide everything we do.

Worship

We gather to honor God with joyful, heartfelt worship.

Community

We build deep relationships through small groups and shared experiences.

Outreach

We serve those in need and share hope beyond our walls.
